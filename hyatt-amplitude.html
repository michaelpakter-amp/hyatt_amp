Synergies

Four places where Hyatt and Amplitude line up

Each came directly from Hyatt's priorities on the call.

Self-serve insight

Ask the data in plain language

What Hyatt shared

Business users rely on complex joins in Power BI and Tableau. Leaders want to ask questions directly, and the data team wants to see what they're asking.

Where Amplitude fits

Global Agent answers in natural language and builds charts on governed data, using Hyatt's own definitions. Admins can review every question asked, and MCP brings the same answers into the AI tools teams already use.

Unlocks: faster answers without new report requests, plus a live record of what leaders care about.

Global Agent · Amplitude MCP · AI Controls
Measure every change

Experimentation as a core principle

What Hyatt shared

Sparkboard gives each business unit its own KPIs behind SSO, with a baseline-versus-change time series for every process change.

Where Amplitude fits

Amplitude Experiment runs randomized tests on the same data model as your analytics, so results tie to the KPIs each unit already watches. Statsig, now part of Amplitude, adds depth for technical teams.

Unlocks: Sparkboard's A/B thinking extended to web, app, and guest-facing changes, with statistical rigor.

Experiment · Feature Flags · Statsig
Survey consolidation

One home for the voice of the guest

What Hyatt shared

Custom guest surveys run through a separate long-standing vendor. Hyatt wants surveys inside general analytics, with more control over quick changes.

Where Amplitude fits

Guides & Surveys lets your team launch and edit in-product surveys directly. AI Feedback pulls in call center notes, support tickets, app reviews, and social posts and sorts them into themes.

  • Spot a friction point, then survey exactly those guests
  • Responses sit next to behavior, loyalty, and booking data

Unlocks: feedback you can act on where you measure it, with fewer tools and faster turnaround.

Guides & Surveys · AI Feedback · Customer Feedback Agent
360° customer quality

The Quality Intelligence Model

What Hyatt shared

Hyatt plans a front end where human observers enter quality scores and comments, combined with survey data and Replicant's automated observation in one model.

Where Amplitude fits

Amplitude's single data model can hold all three inputs as events tied to the same guest or interaction. Agent Analytics scores automated conversations on the same footing as human reviews.

Unlocks: quality scores you can segment by property, channel, or loyalty tier, and trace to the exact moments behind them.
